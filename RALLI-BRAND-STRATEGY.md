# RALLI — Brand Strategy

> *Ralli the troops.*

---

## The Problem

Here's something everyone who's ever planned a trip with friends knows: it shouldn't be this hard.

Someone says "we should go somewhere" and everyone agrees. Then someone has to actually organise it. Chase RSVPs. Answer "what time are we meeting?" fourteen times. Build a spreadsheet nobody reads.

The trip itself is wonderful. The memories last for years. But the planning? The coordinating? That part nobody enjoys.

And here's the thing: people don't remember the logistics. They remember the sunset on that first evening. The joke that became a running gag. The photo everyone laughs at years later.

**The logistics fade. The moments stay.**

---

## The Opportunity

There's no app that properly owns group trips.

- **Airbnb** is about booking, not coordinating
- **Splitwise** handles money, nothing else
- **TripIt** is built for solo travellers
- **WhatsApp** is chaos
- **Google Sheets** is... Google Sheets

Nobody's built something that helps groups plan together, stay in sync during the trip, and keep the memories after.

That's the gap. That's RALLI.

---

## What RALLI Is

**RALLI helps groups go on trips and actually remember them.**

It's not a travel app. It's not a group chat. It's the thing that sits in between — helping you plan, keeping everyone in sync, and holding onto the moments that matter.

### The Core Loop

1. **Plan** — AI helps you build the trip through conversation
2. **Rally** — Get everyone on board, lock in the plan
3. **Go** — Stay in sync while you're there
4. **Remember** — Keep the memories forever

## Who It's For

### The Organiser
Someone always ends up doing the planning. They didn't ask for the job — they just care enough to make it happen. They want the trip to go well. They want everyone to have a good time. And they'd like to enjoy it themselves without burning out.

**What they want:** To look good without the stress.

### Everyone Else
They want to show up, have fun, and know what's happening without reading forty-seven messages in a group chat.

**What they want:** Just tell me where to be.

### The Trips
- Friends going away together
- Stags and hens
- Big birthdays
- Family holidays
- Weddings and events

**The common thread:** A group of people, trying to do something together, who'd rather be present than coordinating.

## The Product

### Planning (AI-Powered)

Not a form. A conversation.

You say "We're thinking Lisbon in September, maybe eight of us." RALLI asks what kind of trip — chilled or more going out? You say "mix, but one big night." RALLI finds you beach spots, restaurants, and somewhere worth going out.

The AI has opinions. "Honestly, the other one's better" is more useful than seventeen options.

### Coordination

- Schedule everyone can see
- Real-time check-ins
- Group chat with context
- Cost splitting built in
- Nudges and reminders

### Fun

- Challenges and games
- Scoreboards
- Custom inside-joke stuff (within reason)

### Memories

- Shared photos, auto-organised
- **Wrapped** — your trip in stats and highlights
- Archive — a memory box to revisit years later

## The AI

Here's what we're not building: a chatbot with a script.

Here's what we are building: the experience of texting a friend who's been everywhere and has good ideas.

### How It Behaves

**Useful but not pushy.** Offers suggestions, answers questions, backs off when ignored.

**Knows when to be brief.** Sometimes you need detail. Sometimes you just need "yes, that place is good."

**Has opinions.** Not arrogant — just helpful. "Trust me on this."

**Sounds like a person.** Reads context, adjusts tone, doesn't repeat itself.

### Stays With You

The AI doesn't disappear after planning. During the trip:

- "Rain tomorrow — here are some backup ideas"
- "You're near a spot that's meant to be great for lunch"
- "Anyone need a taxi? Here's one nearby"

But it knows when to shut up. Nobody wants pings all day.

## Brand Voice

### The Tone

Like a mate, not a brand. Friendly, clear, occasionally funny — but never forcing it.

RALLI doesn't say "utilise" or "leverage" or "your travel experience." It talks like a person.

### Six Principles

**1. Talk like a person, not a company**
"Where are you lot heading?" not "Select your destination."

**2. Be helpful, not keen**
There when you need it, not constantly in your face.

**3. Warmth over hype**
"That's everyone" is better than "LET'S GOOO."

**4. Earn the jokes**
Funny when it lands. Silent when it doesn't.

**5. Know when to be brief**
Detail when it helps. Out of the way when it doesn't.

**6. Have opinions**
Good advice from someone who knows what they're talking about.

### Example Copy

**Onboarding:**
- "Where are you lot heading?"
- "Who's coming? You can add more later."
- "What kind of trip — relaxed, busy, bit of both?"

**In-app:**
- "That's everyone."
- "Tomorrow's looking busy — here's the plan."
- "Dan's already at the restaurant. Classic."

**Wrapped:**
- "Most late to everything: Dave."
- "You ate at that one place four times. No judgement."
- "14 activities. 87 photos. 1 very long night."

## Visual Identity

### The Feeling

Sunset. Beach. Cold drinks. Good music. Everyone's there, no one's stressed. That feeling — easy, warm, golden — that's what RALLI should feel like to use.

### Colours

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|--------|-----|-------|

| Sunset Coral | `#FF6B6B` | Warmth, accents |

| Sand | `#FFF8E7` | Backgrounds |

| Golden Hour | `#FFB347` | Highlights, premium |

### Typography

Rounded, bold, friendly. Poppins, Nunito, Plus Jakarta Sans — that family.

### Imagery

Real photos. Real people. Real moments. Not stock. Not posed.

Think: someone in your group is weirdly good at photography. Golden hour. Candid. Laughter, not posing.

### Motion

Bouncy, snappy, alive. Things should feel good to tap.

## Growth Strategy

### The Wrapped Loop

The Wrapped is the growth engine. People share it. Friends see it. They want one for their trip.

Designed for Instagram, TikTok, group chats. Stats, highlights, inside jokes — naturally shareable.

### Word of Mouth

The organiser tells other organisers. "This app actually made it easy."

Friend groups overlap. One person uses it, brings it to their next trip with different people.

### Beachhead

Start with stags, hens, friend holidays — high energy, lots of photos, natural sharing. Expand from there.
